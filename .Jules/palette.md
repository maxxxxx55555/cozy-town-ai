## 2026-03-02 - Form Completion Feedback & Accessible Labels
**Learning:** Disabling an input field without showing a clear confirmation badge leaves users wondering if their action succeeded. Replacing submitted form controls with a dedicated success chip provides immediate visual delight and reassurance while screen readers benefit from explicit form labels and semantics nodes.
**Action:** Always provide visual confirmation chips and semantics contentDescriptions when form inputs transition to a submitted state.
